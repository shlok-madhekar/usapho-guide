## A moving source squeezes the wavelength

Sound travels at speed $v$ set by the air, regardless of what the source is
doing. A source emitting period $T$ while moving toward you advances $v_s T$
between one crest and the next, so the crests land closer together:

$$\lambda' = (v - v_s)T$$

The observer at rest still receives them at speed $v$, so
$f' = v/\lambda' = f\,v/(v - v_s)$. The wavelength in the medium really has
changed. Photograph the air and you would see it.

## A moving observer meets them faster

Now let the source sit still. The wavelength in the air is untouched at
$\lambda = v/f$. What changes is your closing speed on the crests: moving
toward the source at $v_o$, you sweep past them at $v + v_o$, so

$$f' = \frac{v + v_o}{\lambda} = f\,\frac{v + v_o}{v}$$

Two different mechanisms, two different algebraic forms. Combined:

$$f' = f\,\frac{v \pm v_o}{v \mp v_s}$$

## Why the asymmetry is real

Take $v_s = v_o = v/2$. A source approaching a still observer gives $2f$. An
observer approaching a still source gives $1.5f$. The medium picks out a rest
frame, so "who is moving" is a physically meaningful question — unlike the
relativistic Doppler effect for light, where only relative velocity survives.

Expand both to first order and each gives $f(1 + u/v)$. That is why the
asymmetry is invisible at everyday speeds and why olympiad problems choose
speeds that are not small.

Do not memorize the four sign cases. Ask one question: does the motion bring
them together? If yes the frequency must rise, so pick the signs that shrink
the denominator or grow the numerator. Then substitute.

Only the velocity component along the line joining source and observer counts.
At the instant of closest approach that component is zero and the shift
vanishes — the frequency you hear passes through the true frequency exactly as
the siren goes by, not before.

<Aside label="Where this goes wrong" warn>
  As $v_s \to v$ the denominator goes to zero and the formula diverges. That is
  not a real infinity; it is the formula telling you the crests have piled up
  into a shock front. Beyond $v_s > v$ the expression is meaningless and you
  need the Mach cone with $\sin\theta = v/v_s$ instead.
</Aside>
